      Grudge Bowl

      Twelve bots. One road, tied in a knot.

      Sit back and watch. Three laps, three blind crossroads, three jumps that come down in the wrong place. First car home takes the Bowl, and every bot keeps a list of who hit it on the way there.

      Click a name on the board to follow that bot. Keys 1 to 6 pick a camera, A hands it back to the director, Space pauses.
